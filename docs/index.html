PrepareMe

An offline preparedness reference library for iPhone and iPad.

  • Guides for power outages, storms, water, first aid and more, readable with no internet
  • Search that works offline, by word or by meaning
  • An offline street map with your own pins
  • A household inventory with expiry reminders
  • A locked store for essential documents, opened with Face ID or your passcode

Getting started

Open the app and tap Try the Sample to see how it works with a built-in sample library. Your own library comes from the PrepareMe companion on a Mac you run at home, over your home Wi-Fi, or from a library folder you add with Add from Files. Once it is on the device, everything works with no internet.

PrepareMe is reference material, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
